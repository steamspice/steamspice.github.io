Our story

Made the way we learnt it.

I have fond memories of cooking momo with my family in Nepal, and I missed those moments when I was living abroad. Now I make momo by hand at home, with fresh ingredients, the way I learnt from my family, so others who crave momo can enjoy them too.

The aim is simple: momo at home at a manageable cost, without the hassle, the time or the expense of a restaurant.

Made by hand at home, with fresh ingredients.

Wherever home is now

For anyone far from home, a bag of momo in the freezer is a little closer.

New to momo? Think of a dumpling, steamed until soft, with a sauce that wakes everything up.

Questions

Good to know

How do I order?

Choose your packs in the shop, pick a pickup day and time, and send your order. It is not final until we message you to confirm it.

Do you deliver?

No. We do not deliver. Orders are pickup only, in Leicester.

How do I pay?

You pay cash when you pick up your order. There is no payment on the website.

What comes with my order?

Each pack has 40 momo, frozen. One free chutney pot comes with every order, and you can add extra pots to your cart.

Are they ready to eat?

Not straight from the bag. They come frozen and are ready to steam at home when you want them. The how to cook page has the steps.

Where do I collect?

In Leicester. We send the exact address on WhatsApp once we confirm your order.
