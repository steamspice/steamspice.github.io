Privacy notice

What we do with the details you give us when you order.

Who we are

Steam & Spice sells homemade frozen momo for pickup in the UK. You can reach us on +44 7413 069241.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, your phone number, the items you order, the pickup day and time you choose, and any note you add. We do not ask for payment details: you pay cash when you pick up.

Why we use it

We use these details only to prepare your order, to hand it over at pickup and to message you about it, for example to confirm it or to say it is ready. We may message you by WhatsApp, by text message or by phone call.

Where it is kept and who can see it

Order details are stored in a database run by our service provider Supabase. They are visible to the shop through a private, password-protected admin area. When we message you, the messaging service you use (such as WhatsApp or your phone's text service) handles that message under its own terms.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use analytics or advertising cookies and does not track you across other sites. Your cart is kept in your own browser storage on your device so it is still there when you come back, and the receipt page uses your browser to show the order you have just placed. Nothing else is stored on your device by the shop pages.

How long we keep it

We keep order details only as long as we need them to run the shop and to meet our legal duties.

Your rights

You can ask us what details we hold about you, ask us to correct them, or ask us to delete them, by contacting us on the phone number above. If you are unhappy with how we have used your details you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
